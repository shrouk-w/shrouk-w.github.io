Privacy policy

Drinkder for Android · Effective November 2, 2026
Przeczytaj po polsku

Drinkder is provided by Kacper Wierzba. This policy explains how the app and this website handle information. For privacy questions, contact kacper.wierzbaa@gmail.com.

1. Accounts and information on your device

Drinkder does not require a Drinkder account and does not operate its own account or analytics server. Your saved cocktail IDs, ingredient list, selected background, unlocked backgrounds and a cached indication of your verified remove-ads purchase are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Cocktail and ingredient information and images may be cached to make the app work faster.

These preferences are not uploaded to a Drinkder-operated server. Android may include app data in system backup or device transfer, depending on your device and Google backup settings. Your ingredient names are sent to the recipe service when you use ingredient-based search.

2. Cocktail searches and images

Drinkder requests recipes, ingredient lists and images from TheCocktailDB. Depending on the feature, these requests include cocktail names or IDs and ingredient names you search for. Like other internet services, the provider receives your IP address and technical request information. Drinkder does not request access to contacts, precise device location, the camera or microphone.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Drinkder integrates Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform. The app can show native ads between cocktail cards and optional rewarded ads to unlock backgrounds. Test releases may use Google’s demo ads instead of live advertising.

When the advertising SDK communicates with Google, it can collect or share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), advertising or other device/account identifiers, ad and app interaction information, and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, service operation and fraud prevention. Personalization and data use depend on the ad configuration, your choices, region and device settings. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

Where required, a consent form is presented before live ads are requested. When applicable, the app’s settings provide a privacy-options entry to revisit your advertising choices. You can also manage advertising IDs in Android settings. Optional rewarded ads are shown only after you choose to watch an ad; the background is unlocked after the advertising SDK confirms the reward.

A verified “Remove ads” purchase disables new ad requests by the app and unlocks all backgrounds. It does not delete records already held by Google. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Payments

One-time purchases are processed by Google Play. Drinkder receives purchase information needed to verify the product, check purchase status, acknowledge the transaction and restore access. The app stores a local cache of the verified entitlement and checks ownership with Google Play. Drinkder does not receive or store your payment-card details. Google manages your Google account, payment records and purchase history under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Support messages and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and resolve support issues, and retain it only as necessary for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Local app data remains until you remove it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app; system backups may remain subject to your backup settings. Google and TheCocktailDB retain information they receive under their own practices and applicable requirements.

6. Your choices and rights

You can remove favorites or ingredients in the app, clear app storage in Android settings, manage Android backup settings, and change advertising choices where available. There is no Drinkder account to delete. Clearing local data or uninstalling does not cancel or erase a Google Play purchase; purchases and refund requests are managed through Google Play.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us about information we control; requests concerning Google or other providers may need to be addressed to them directly. Where consent is required for optional advertising processing, it can be withdrawn through the available privacy controls. Other processing is used to provide requested features, support, security and required transaction handling.

7. Security and international services

The app’s configured recipe requests use HTTPS, and Google’s SDKs use encrypted network connections. Local preferences use Android’s app-private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party services may process information outside your country under their own safeguards and privacy policies.

8. Intended audience

Drinkder is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address the request.

9. This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms or cookies to it. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical access information to provide and secure hosting. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following external links is subject to the destination service’s practices.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date appears above. Contact: Kacper Wierzba, kacper.wierzbaa@gmail.com.
